# Morocco's 2026 elections: briefing (25 Sept 2026)

**Vote held:** Wednesday 23 September 2026. All 395 seats in the House of Representatives: 305 constituency seats plus 90 regional-list seats reserved for women.
**Status:** provisional results announced 24 Sept by Interior Minister Abdelouafi Laftit. They become final after the Constitutional Court rules on any appeals.

## Results (provisional)
| Party | 2026 | 2021 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authenticity and Modernity (PAM) | **97** | 87 | +10 |
| National Rally of Independents (RNI) | 66 | 102 | −36 |
| Istiqlal | 65 | 81 | −16 |
| Justice and Development (PJD) | 54 | 13 | +41 |
| Popular Movement (MP) | 29 | | |
| Socialist Union (USFP) | 26 | | |
| Constitutional Union (UC) | 17 | | |
| Others (incl. PPS) | ~41 | | |

- **Majority:** 198 seats. No party is close to that on its own.
- **Turnout:** **38.02%** (about 6 million voters), down from 50.35% in 2021. That's the second-lowest since 1999, after 2007's ~37%.

## Key takeaways
1. **PAM comes first**, so it gets to lead coalition talks. Under the constitution, King Mohammed VI appoints the head of government from the largest party. Names mentioned:
   - **Fatima-Zahra El Mansouri**, PAM leader. She would be only the second woman to head an Arab government, after Tunisia's Najla Bouden.
   - **Fouzi Lekjaa**, budget minister and head of the Royal Moroccan Football Federation.
2. **The RNI slumps**, from 102 to 66 seats. Outgoing head of government Aziz Akhannouch stepped down as RNI leader in February 2026 and was replaced by Mohamed Chaouki. He was the main target of the 2025 Gen Z 212 protests.
3. **The PJD makes a comeback**, from 13 to 54 seats. Leader Abdelilah Benkirane calls it a "great victory". He all but rules out joining a PAM-led government and plans to fight Morocco's electoral rules from opposition.
4. **Turnout fell to 38%.** Low participation reflects the same grievances as the Gen Z 212 protests (27 Sept to 18 Oct 2025): youth unemployment, inequality, poor health and education services, and anger at spending on World Cup stadiums.
5. **The vote came amid a Ceuta crisis.** It followed a deadly mass migration attempt at the Spanish enclave of Ceuta. The European Parliament has since held Morocco responsible.
6. **Observers:** a Council of Europe delegation said the vote ran smoothly but flagged the low turnout.

## Coalition arithmetic (my calculation from the seat totals)
- **Outgoing trio (PAM, RNI, Istiqlal):** 228 seats, a comfortable majority. But PAM leaders have ruled out another partnership with the RNI.
- **Without the RNI:** PAM + Istiqlal + MP + UC = 208. PAM + Istiqlal + MP + USFP = 217. Both work.
- **PAM + PJD:** unlikely. The two parties are longtime rivals, and the PJD says it will stay in opposition.
- El Mansouri: "no party with which we refuse to sit down". Talks have not formally begun.

## What to watch
- The King's choice of head of government, likely within days.
- Whether PAM builds a coalition without the RNI.
- Constitutional Court rulings on appeals.
- Whether the PJD turns its comeback into a strong opposition ahead of 2031.
- How the new government answers the Gen Z agenda: jobs, health and education.
