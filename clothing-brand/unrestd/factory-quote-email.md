# Factory quote email

Send this to three factories that take 20 to 50 pieces per design, with `tech-pack-shift-jacket.pdf` attached. Pay for a sample before you pay for a batch, and pay overseas factories through an escrow or trade-assurance service.

```
Subject: Quote request: cropped duck canvas work jacket, 40 units, tech pack attached

Hi [name],

I'm launching UNRESTD, a US streetwear brand, and I'm looking for a factory
for a cropped 12 oz duck canvas work jacket. The tech pack is attached
(flats, size chart, bill of materials, labels).

First order: 3 proto samples (one per colour, size L), then 40 units
(Black 24, Duck Tan 16) across sizes S to XXL.

Could you send me:
1. Sample price and sample lead time
2. Unit price at 40 units and at 100 units
3. Bulk lead time after the sample is approved
4. Shipping to [city], USA, and whether duties are included (DDP)
5. Your minimum order per colour, and whether you can source the 12 oz
   duck, blanket lining, #5 brass zips and the woven labels
6. Photos of similar jackets you have made

Thank you,
[Your name]
UNRESTD · unrestd.com
```
