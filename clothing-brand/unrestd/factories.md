## Which to use for what

- **Shift Jacket, lot 001:** get quotes from **Mike Apparel, ZipaSports and Huaxing**. Mike Apparel names the exact jacket style and fabric range. ZipaSports has the lowest minimum. Huaxing does the washes for Faded Black and Washed Grey.
- **Night Shift Jacket (hooded):** **ZipaSports** already makes a hooded duck canvas jacket with a quilted hood and rib cuffs. Ask for 12 oz (about 400 gsm) duck, because their standard is 320 gsm.
- **Drop 02 trucker jacket:** **ZipaSports** makes 12 oz denim truckers with a snap front and chest flap pockets. Ask for the same build in duck canvas.
- **When you scale to 100+ per colour:** **VANRD**.
- **Heavyweight cut-and-sew hoodies:** **White Cotton** (Portugal) for premium quality, or Mike Apparel for a lower price.

## Things to ask every factory

1. **Is the 50-piece minimum per design, or per colour?** Lot 001 is 24 black and 16 tan.
2. **What's the fabric weight?** You want 12 oz (about 400 gsm) 100% cotton duck. Ask for a swatch or a photo of the roll label.
3. **What's the total delivered price** (DDP to your city) at 40 and at 100 units?
4. **Can I see photos or a video of canvas work jackets you've made** for other brands?
5. **What are the payment terms?** Staged payments are normal. Wearable Apparels, for example, publishes 40% on confirmation, 30% at production and 30% before shipment.

## Checklist before you pay anyone

- Do a **live video call** of the factory floor. Mike Apparel, ZipaSports and Wearable Apparels all offer one.
- **Pay for one sample first**, and never pay 100% up front for bulk.
- Check they're a real registered business. Sialkot factories should be members of the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce (SCCI) and registered with Pakistan's tax authority (FBR). Ask for the certificates.
- Get the **price, fabric, timeline and defect policy in writing**, and have both sides sign the tech pack.
- Pay by bank wire in stages, or through an escrow or trade-assurance service. Don't pay through gift cards or personal payment apps.
- Wash the sample three times and measure it against the size chart before you approve bulk.
