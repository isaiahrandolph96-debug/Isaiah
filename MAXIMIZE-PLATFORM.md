*Text only. Built from the 26 September 2026 stories. Every example is ready to copy.*

## 1. Stronger stories: before and after

### A. Add a real human voice
**Before** (today's Ethiopia narration, opening):
> In northern Ethiopia, the phones have gone silent again.

**After:**
> "There is an atmosphere of fear." That's how Selam Fantay, who sells garlic in the market in Mekelle, describes her city this week. In northern Ethiopia, the phones have gone silent again.

*Why it works:* A named person with a real quote (from AP) turns a war into someone's Friday. Use one sourced voice per story, never an invented one.

### B. Tell returning viewers what's new
**Before:** every day's Ethiopia narration re-explains the whole war.

**After** (the opening of tomorrow's version):
> Day five of Ethiopia's new war. Since yesterday: [new development]. If you're just joining us, here's what you need to know in 20 seconds…

*Why it works:* Daily followers get news, not repeats, and new viewers still get the background. It trains people to come back each day.

### C. "Explain like I'm new" lines
Drop one plain line into each story the first time a place or group appears:
- **Tigray:** "a region in Ethiopia's far north, home to about six million people."
- **JNIM:** "a coalition of armed groups in West Africa that is linked to al-Qaeda."
- **RSF:** "a paramilitary force that grew out of militias in Sudan's Darfur region, now at war with the army."
- **The Sahel:** "the dry belt of land just south of the Sahara, stretching from Senegal to Sudan."
- **El Niño:** "a warming of the Pacific Ocean every few years that shifts weather around the world."

*Correction:* When I suggested these ideas earlier, I said Tigray is "about the size of Portugal". That was wrong. Portugal is roughly twice as big. Use the population line above instead.

### D. A fact-check pass before posting (example: Sudan)
| Claim in the script | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The US didn't issue Burhan a visa for the UN General Assembly | The National, Al Jazeera, Sudan Tribune | ✅ Confirmed by several outlets |
| The visa was tied to signing a 90-day ceasefire | A Sudanese diplomatic source and a Western analyst (Sudan Tribune) | ⚠️ Single-source; keep "according to…" |
| Burhan refused | Same sources | ⚠️ Keep "reportedly" |
| Guterres expressed regret | Middle East Monitor, UN spokesperson | ✅ |
| About half a million people trapped in El Obeid | Al Jazeera (July 2026) | ⚠️ Dated; say "have been trapped", or update |
| The war began in April 2023 | Widely reported | ✅ |

*Why it works:* This table takes two minutes and protects the brand. One wrong number can cost more followers than ten good posts win.

## 2. One story, eight posts (example: the Sudan visa story)
Post one story on every platform, each in its own style.

**1. Instagram Reel caption:**
> Sudan's leader was meant to speak to the world at the UN. The US kept him out. 🇸🇩
>
> 🛂 No visa issued for General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan
> 🕊️ Reportedly tied to signing a 90-day ceasefire, which he refused
> ⚔️ Meanwhile the war that began in April 2023 goes on
>
> Should visas be used to push for peace? Tell us below. 👇
> #AfricaBlindSpot #Sudan #UNGA #Africa

**2. Pinned comment:**
> Sources: Sudan Tribune · Al Jazeera (24 Sept) · The National (22 Sept) · Middle East Monitor (25 Sept). Facts as of 26 Sept 2026.

**3. TikTok / Shorts title:**
> Sudan's leader was kept out of the UN. Here's why. 🇸🇩

**4. X / Threads (3 posts):**
> 1/ Sudan's army chief, Gen. al-Burhan, was due to address the UN this week. The US never issued his visa.
>
> 2/ Sources say the visa was tied to one condition: sign a 90-day ceasefire. He reportedly refused. Sudan says barring a head of state breaks the UN host agreement.
>
> 3/ Meanwhile the war that began in April 2023 grinds on. Hundreds of thousands have been trapped in El Obeid. [link]

**5. Instagram Story poll:**
> Should the US use visas to pressure leaders into ceasefires? YES / NO

**6. Instagram Story quiz:**
> When did Sudan's current war begin? 2019 / **April 2023** ✅ / 2025

**7. LinkedIn:**
> A visa, not a vote, may be the latest lever in Sudan's war. The US withheld General al-Burhan's visa for the UN General Assembly, reportedly over a 90-day ceasefire proposal. Whatever you think of the tactic, it shows how diplomacy around Africa's largest humanitarian crisis is shifting. Sources in the comments.

**8. WhatsApp Channel or newsletter (2 lines):**
> 🇸🇩 Sudan: the US withheld army chief al-Burhan's UN visa, reportedly over a ceasefire demand. The war goes on.
> Full story: [link]

*Why it works:* One piece of research becomes eight posts. Each platform gets content that looks native to it, not a copy-paste.

## 3. Closing lines that match the tone
Always end with a question, a call to action, or both. Match it to the severity tier.
| Tier | Example story | Closing line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Critical | Ethiopia war | "The last time Tigray went dark, famine followed. Send this to someone who hasn't heard." |
| 1 · Critical | Mali | "What would you want the world to do? Tell us below." |
| 2 · Serious | Ebola | "Share this so someone you love knows the facts, not the fear." |
| 2 · Serious | South Africa GBV | "Check on the women in your life today." (plus the helpline) |
| 3 · Developing | Morocco election | "Would you have voted? Yes or no in the comments." |
| 4 · Opportunity | Nigeria minerals | "Who should benefit most from Africa's minerals? Tell us." |
| 5 · Human interest | Kenya v Eritrea | "Drop your score prediction below. ⚽" |

**Rule:** No jokes, emojis or "🔥" on tier 1–2 stories. Use emojis freely on tiers 4–5.

| **Saturday** | *Africa This Week* (a roundup) | The top 5 stories in 60 seconds |

**Daily:** the 10 narrations. Post 2–3 as Reels, turn the others into Stories and carousels, and keep the rest as backup content.

## 6. Growth moves beyond posting
1. **Collaborate with creators.** Use Instagram's "Collab" post feature with African creators, diaspora pages and student groups. One collab can reach an audience the size of your whole following.
2. **Reply with videos.** Answer the best comment question with a short narration reply. It turns commenters into regulars.
3. **Credit local journalists.** Tag and credit reporters from Premium Times, Daily Maverick, Addis Standard and others. They reshare, and it builds credibility.
4. **Own your keywords.** Use the same hashtag set every time (#AfricaBlindSpot plus 3–5 topic tags) so the algorithm learns your niche.
5. **Build a WhatsApp Channel.** Much of Africa gets its news on WhatsApp. Post a daily 5-line briefing there.
6. **Publish a correction when needed.** If a fact changes, post a short update ("Update: …"). Audiences trust pages that correct themselves.
