## Instagram / TikTok caption
Six hundred years to build. Nine days to fall. 🌍

In southern Nigeria, the Edo people dug walls of red earth by hand for six centuries. They ran 16,000 km around the Kingdom of Benin, the longest earthworks ever dug without machines.

In 1897, a British army took Benin City in nine days. The palace burned, and thousands of treasures (the Benin Bronzes) were carried off to Europe.

The walls are still there. Most of the world has never heard of them.

Did your school ever teach you this? 👇
