YASMINE'S VOTE (60-SECOND CUT)
A short film for Africa Blind Spot
Runtime: 60 seconds · Vertical 9:16 · Instagram Reel · Dramatization based on real events

Small on-screen note for the first 3 seconds: "Dramatization. Characters are fictional. Events and figures are real."


FADE IN:

EXT. ROOFTOP, CASABLANCA - DAWN                                  [0:00-0:06]

YASMINE (24), in a hoodie, sits on the ledge turning a voter card in her fingers. Cranes stand over a half-built stadium in the distance.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
          Yasmine is twenty-four. Engineering
          degree. No job. Today, Morocco votes.

ON SCREEN: 23 SEPT 2026 · ELECTION DAY


EXT. BOULEVARD - NIGHT - ONE YEAR EARLIER                        [0:06-0:12]

A crowd of young marchers, phone lights raised. Yasmine holds a sign: "HOSPITALS, NOT STADIUMS."

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
          A year ago, she marched with Gen Z 212,
          for jobs, hospitals and schools.

                    YASMINE
               (shouting)
          Hospitals, not stadiums!


INT. FAMILY APARTMENT - MORNING                                  [0:12-0:21]

Mint tea on the table. HAJ AHMED (74), her grandfather, buttons his best djellaba.

                    HAJ AHMED
          Shoes on. We're voting.

                    YASMINE
          What changes?

                    HAJ AHMED
          The King picks the head of government
          from the party with the most seats.
          So change the seats.


EXT. POLLING STATION - DAY                                       [0:21-0:27]

Rows of empty voting booths. Haj Ahmed walks in. Yasmine stops at the gate and turns away.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
          He voted. She walked home. So did
          six in ten Moroccans.

ON SCREEN: TURNOUT 38% (2021: 50%)


INT. FAMILY APARTMENT - NIGHT - NEXT DAY                         [0:27-0:37]

The TV news reads the results. Haj Ahmed leans in; Yasmine watches from the doorway.

ON SCREEN: PAM 97 · RNI 66 · ISTIQLAL 65 · PJD 54

                    HAJ AHMED
          PAM first!

                    YASMINE
          And Akhannouch's party crashed. A
          hundred and two seats down to sixty-six.

                    HAJ AHMED
               (laughing)
          And the Islamists are back. Thirteen
          to fifty-four!


INT. FAMILY APARTMENT - LATER                                    [0:37-0:46]

Haj Ahmed lines up sugar cubes on the tea tray like a parliament and slides piles together.

                    HAJ AHMED
          Three hundred ninety-five seats. You
          need one ninety-eight. PAM has
          ninety-seven.

                    YASMINE
          So who joins them?

                    HAJ AHMED
          Not the R-N-I, they say.

ON SCREEN: NO COALITION AGREED YET


INT. FAMILY APARTMENT - CONTINUOUS                               [0:46-0:51]

On TV: PAM leader FATIMA-ZAHRA EL MANSOURI at a podium (news footage).

                    YASMINE
               (quietly)
          If the King picks her, she'd be our
          first woman head of government.


EXT. ROOFTOP, CASABLANCA - NIGHT                                 [0:51-0:58]

The stadium site blazes with floodlights; a hospital beside it sits dim. Yasmine looks at her voter card and puts it in her pocket instead of throwing it away.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
          New parliament. Same questions. And
          it barely made the news. That's the
          blind spot. Send this to someone who
          hasn't heard.

FADE OUT.
