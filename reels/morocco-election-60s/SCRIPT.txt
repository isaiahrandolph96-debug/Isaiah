AFRICA BLIND SPOT | REEL SCRIPT
"Morocco just voted. Most people didn't."
Series: Blind Spot of the Week | Length: ~55 s | 11 scenes | Format: 9:16 Instagram Reel
Facts as of: provisional results, 24 September 2026

======================================================================
SCENE 1 | HOOK (0:00-0:05)
HEADLINE:   6 IN 10 MOROCCANS / DIDN'T VOTE        (gold: DIDN'T VOTE)
VOICEOVER:  "Morocco just held an election. And more than six in ten voters stayed home."
ANIMATION:  An empty polling station at dawn: rows of ballot booths and one lone voter.
            A turnout gauge drains from 50% (2021) down to 38%.

======================================================================
SCENE 2 | CONTEXT (0:05-0:10)
HEADLINE:   A KINGDOM / THAT VOTES                  (gold: THAT VOTES)
VOICEOVER:  "Morocco is a kingdom, but its three-hundred-and-ninety-five-seat parliament is elected every five years."
CAPTION:    "Morocco is a kingdom, but its 395-seat parliament is elected every five years."
ANIMATION:  Map zooms from Africa to northern Morocco. Rabat and Casablanca pulse.
            (Frame on the north so no disputed border is drawn.)

======================================================================
SCENE 3 | THE RULE (0:10-0:15)
HEADLINE:   THE RULE THAT / DECIDES EVERYTHING      (gold: DECIDES EVERYTHING)
VOICEOVER:  "The King picks the head of government from the party that wins the most seats."
ANIMATION:  A gold crown above a parliament half-circle. A beam of light searches the seats
            and lands on the biggest block.

======================================================================
SCENE 4 | THE WINNER (0:15-0:20)
HEADLINE:   PAM / COMES FIRST                       (gold: PAM); counts up to "97 SEATS"
VOICEOVER:  "This time, that's PAM, with ninety-seven seats, up from eighty-seven."
CAPTION:    "This time, that's PAM, with 97 seats, up from 87."
ANIMATION:  The parliament half-circle fills seat by seat in party colours, PAM's 97 in gold.

======================================================================
SCENE 5 | THE FALL (0:20-0:25)
HEADLINE:   THE RULING PARTY / CRASHED              (gold: CRASHED)
VOICEOVER:  "The R-N-I, which led the government, fell from one hundred and two seats to sixty-six."
CAPTION:    "The RNI, which led the government, fell from 102 seats to 66."
ANIMATION:  Before/after bars shrinking: RNI 102 to 66, Istiqlal 81 to 65.

======================================================================
SCENE 6 | THE COMEBACK (0:25-0:31)
HEADLINE:   THE ISLAMISTS / ARE BACK                (gold: ARE BACK)
VOICEOVER:  "The Islamist P-J-D quadrupled its seats, from thirteen to fifty-four, and says it will stay in opposition."
CAPTION:    "The Islamist PJD quadrupled its seats, from 13 to 54, and says it will stay in opposition."
ANIMATION:  A single bar grows from 13 to 54 with a "x4" chip.

======================================================================
SCENE 7 | WHY SO ANGRY? (0:31-0:37)
HEADLINE:   WHY SO / ANGRY?                         (gold: ANGRY?)
VOICEOVER:  "Last year, Gen Z protesters marched for jobs, hospitals and schools, not World Cup stadiums."
ANIMATION:  A silhouetted crowd marches across the frame with "212" placards. A football stadium
            glows in the distance while a hospital and a school sit dark.

======================================================================
SCENE 8 | THE PUZZLE (0:37-0:43)
HEADLINE:   THE COALITION / PUZZLE                  (gold: PUZZLE)
VOICEOVER:  "PAM needs about one hundred more seats, and it has ruled out its old partner, the R-N-I."
CAPTION:    "PAM needs about 100 more seats, and it has ruled out its old partner, the RNI."
ANIMATION:  The half-circle again, with a majority line at 198. Istiqlal, Popular Movement and
            Constitutional Union slide in next to PAM and cross the line (208). The RNI block is greyed out.

======================================================================
SCENE 9 | THE HUMAN ANGLE (0:43-0:48)
HEADLINE:   A WOMAN / AT THE TOP?                   (gold: AT THE TOP?)
VOICEOVER:  "If the King picks PAM leader Fatima-Zahra El Mansouri, she'd be the Arab world's second woman to lead a government."
ANIMATION:  A single chair at the head of a long table under a spotlight, with a crown motif above.

======================================================================
SCENE 10 | THE BLIND SPOT (0:48-0:52)
HEADLINE:   BARELY / MADE THE NEWS                  (gold: MADE THE NEWS)
VOICEOVER:  "Morocco just reshaped its parliament, and it barely made the news. That's the blind spot."
ANIMATION:  The doom-scroll phone feed. A gold "Morocco election" card slides past unnoticed.

======================================================================
SCENE 11 | CTA (0:52-0:55)
HEADLINE:   SEND THIS TO / SOMEONE WHO HASN'T HEARD
VOICEOVER:  "Send this to someone who hasn't heard."   (reuse the existing CTA clip)
ANIMATION:  Paper planes fly out from Morocco across Africa, then the brand end card.
