FORMAT KEY

SUPER = on-screen text. The brand header "AFRICA BLIND SPOT" (gold, small, top centre) stays on screen throughout.
HEADLINE = two lines, bold uppercase, centred; the [bracketed] line is gold, the other white.
CAPTIONS = word-by-word subtitles near the bottom; each word turns gold as it is spoken.
GFX = motion graphics. B-ROLL = real footage alternative, if you shoot or license footage instead.
Keep all text out of the bottom 360 px and the right-hand 120 px (Instagram buttons).

PRODUCTION NOTES

VOICE
- One narrator: calm, authoritative, documentary tone (ours: ElevenLabs voice "Yusuf Hakeem Kiser").
- Read party acronyms letter by letter: "R-N-I", "P-J-D". Say "PAM" as a word ("pam").
- Pace: about 2.6 words per second. Leave about 0.2 s between scenes.
- Master the voice to -16 LUFS. Keep the music about 12 dB under the voice and duck it when the narrator speaks.

LOOK
- Palette: near-black backgrounds, warm amber light, gold #D4AA34, off-white #F6F4EE.
- Font: a heavy geometric sans (we use DejaVu Sans Bold; Montserrat ExtraBold works too).
- Headlines pop in (fade plus scale from 86% to 100% over 0.3 s) at the start of each scene.
- 0.2 s crossfades between scenes. A thin gold progress bar runs along the bottom edge.
- Slow push-in (100% to 110%) on every background.

PARTY COLOURS (graphics only; not official party colours)
PAM gold · RNI blue · Istiqlal red · PJD green · Popular Movement purple · USFP pink · Constitutional Union light blue · Others grey.

FACT SHEET (for on-screen accuracy)
- Election day: 23 September 2026. Provisional results announced 24 Sept by Interior Minister Abdelouafi Laftit.
- 395 seats (305 constituency + 90 women's regional lists). Majority = 198.
- PAM 97 (2021: 87) · RNI 66 (102) · Istiqlal 65 (81) · PJD 54 (13) · Popular Movement 29 · USFP 26 · Constitutional Union 17 · others ~41.
- Turnout 38.02% (2021: 50.35%).
- The coalition in scene 8 is ONE arithmetically possible majority, labelled "not yet agreed". Update it if a real coalition is announced before you post.
- If the King names a head of government before posting, rewrite scene 9.
