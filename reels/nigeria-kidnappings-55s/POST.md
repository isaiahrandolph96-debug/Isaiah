## LinkedIn
Mass kidnapping has become one of northern Nigeria's most profitable businesses. SBM Intelligence estimates ransom payments to armed groups more than tripled in a year, to about 7.8 billion naira (nearly $6 million). Last weekend alone, more than 80 people, mostly women farmers, were taken in two attacks in Niger and Zamfara states (AFP). Harvest season is when rural families are most exposed, and every abduction puts food security at risk too. With a presidential election in January, security is on the ballot.
