## LinkedIn
Ethiopia, Africa's second most populous country, is on the edge of a wider war. On 23 September, Tigrayan forces seized three airports; days earlier, seven armed groups formed an alliance to topple the federal government. The 2022 Pretoria agreement, which ended a war the African Union's mediator estimates may have killed up to 600,000 people, is near collapse.

Why it matters beyond Ethiopia: the Horn of Africa sits on one of the world's busiest trade routes, borders war-torn Sudan, and risks drawing in Eritrea. For businesses, humanitarian agencies and policymakers, this is a story to watch now, not when it is already a crisis.
