## LinkedIn
A small plane carrying a Congolese army delegation crashed into a residential area of Kenge, DR Congo, on 25 September after developing a technical fault. It had nowhere to land. All 13–14 people on board died. Congo depends on air travel to connect a country the size of Western Europe with few paved roads, yet its aviation safety record is among the world's worst: for years the EU has barred almost all Congolese airlines from its airspace. Safer skies are an infrastructure and governance question, not just an aviation one. Sources in the comments.
