## X / Threads thread
1. Only ~1 in 20 people in South Sudan has electricity. Uganda has just started building a power line to change that. 🧵
2. The African Development Fund is putting in ~$121M for Uganda's section.
3. The line runs ~299 km at 400 kV, from Olwiyo in northern Uganda, through Nimule, to Juba. It can carry up to 1,000 MW.
4. It also plugs South Sudan into the Eastern Africa Power Pool, where countries trade electricity across borders.
5. Construction runs 2026–2029. Africa building its own future rarely trends. That's the blind spot. [Reel link]

## LinkedIn
Uganda has begun implementing the South Sudan–Uganda Power Interconnection Project: ~299 km of 400 kV line, up to 1,000 MW, backed by ~$121M from the African Development Fund. It links one of the world's least-electrified countries (~5% access) to the Eastern Africa Power Pool. Target completion: 2029. #Energy #Infrastructure #Africa

## Notes on the numbers
- **$121M** is the African Development Fund financing for **Uganda's component** (UA 91.7M). The total project is about $260M, and the ADF approved $153.66M overall in Dec 2024, including a $32.5M grant to South Sudan.
- **"1 in 20"** comes from the World Bank's 2023 figure of 5.4% of South Sudanese with electricity access.
- Uganda's section is ~150 km (Olwiyo–Bibia–border). South Sudan's is ~149 km (Nimule–Gumbo/Juba).
