# Mali, "Silenced": CapCut production guide

**Narration file:** `MALI-NARRATION-CapCut.txt` (about 1,750 words; 11–12 min at a normal pace). Facts as of 25 Sept 2026.

## How to use it in CapCut
1. **One long video (YouTube or Facebook):** paste each PART into CapCut in order (Text → Text to speech, or Script to video). Delete the `=== PART ===` lines first so they aren't read aloud.
2. **A series (Instagram, TikTok):** each PART is a stand-alone episode of 1–2.5 min. Post them as "Mali, Part 1 of 7", and so on.
3. **Voice:** pick a calm, deep news voice. Set speed to 0.95–1.0 so the numbers land.
4. **Captions:** Auto captions, in the house style: gold for the active word, white for the rest, bold sans, bottom third but above the app UI.
5. **Music:** a low, tense ambient bed at about 15–20% volume, ducked under the voice. Add a soft whoosh on each part change.
6. **Brand:** the "AFRICA BLIND SPOT" gold header throughout. Add the end card after Part 7 (or after each episode in a series).

## Editorial rules for this video
- **Name the source on screen** for every death toll (security source, diplomatic source, JNIM claim, HRW, UN).
- **Never use JNIM videos or images** of captured soldiers. They are propaganda, and showing captives can violate their dignity.
- **Use no graphic imagery** of victims.
- **Label** the Ke-Macina report "unconfirmed".
- **Keep the government's view in Part 7.** It's what makes this journalism, not advocacy.
- **Use only licensed footage:** CapCut's stock library, your own maps and graphics, or clips you have rights to.
