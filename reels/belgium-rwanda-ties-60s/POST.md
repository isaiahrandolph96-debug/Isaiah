## X / Threads thread
1. Rwanda and Belgium had no diplomatic ties for 18 months. On 22 Sept they agreed to restore them and reopen embassies. How did it get that bad? 🧵
2. It's about eastern Congo. In early 2025, the M23 rebel group seized Goma and Bukavu, two major cities. UN experts say Rwanda backs M23. Rwanda denies it.
3. Belgium, which ruled Rwanda until 1962, led calls in Europe for sanctions. In Feb 2025 Rwanda scrapped a €95M Belgian aid deal.
4. On 17 March 2025 Rwanda cut ties and gave Belgian diplomats 48 hours to leave. Belgium expelled Rwanda's diplomats in return.
5. The thaw began in Doha, Qatar, in June 2025. This week, at the UN in New York, the two foreign ministers announced the restart.
6. Meanwhile more than 5 million people in eastern Congo have been forced from their homes. That's the blind spot. [Reel link]

## LinkedIn
Rwanda and Belgium have restored diplomatic relations, 18 months after Kigali severed them over Belgium's stance on the conflict in eastern DR Congo. The joint statement by foreign ministers Olivier Nduhungirehe and Maxime Prévot (22 Sept 2026) commits both sides to "open, candid, and transparent dialogue", credits Qatar for hosting their first meeting in June 2025, and says embassies will reopen, first under chargés d'affaires. The break had followed Rwanda ending a €95M five-year Belgian development programme and EU sanctions over the M23 rebellion. Diplomacy is back; the displacement crisis in eastern Congo (5M+ people) is not over. #Diplomacy #Africa #Rwanda #Belgium #DRC

## Notes on the numbers
- **18 months:** 17 March 2025 (ties severed) to 22 Sept 2026 (restored).
- **€95 million:** the value of the five-year Belgian development programme Rwanda ended on 18 Feb 2025 (Euronews; Beloff).
- **48 hours:** Rwanda's foreign ministry statement, 17 March 2025.
- **5M+ displaced:** Global Centre for R2P gives 5.3 million in eastern DRC; IOM DTM says over 5 million in North Kivu, South Kivu and Ituri. **18.5M in need** is IOM's 2026 figure for all of DR Congo.
- **M23 and Rwanda:** attributed to UN experts, with Rwanda's denial stated in the same line.
- **Belgian rule until 1962:** Rwanda became independent on 1 July 1962 (Britannica).
